# About Leo Noel Zuze

## What he does

His current specialization is AI agents, RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) pipelines, and AI-powered workflows — building systems that can reason over real data and take action, not just respond to prompts.

He is 20 years old.

Everything he's building now — technical depth in AI agents and RAG, full-stack product experience, and early ventures like DANN — is groundwork for that.
